Privacy Policy

Effective: July 25, 2026

1. Our Belief

We believe everyone deserves 100% privacy. Dr.Exam is designed to collect only the information necessary to provide the Service while keeping your data as secure as reasonably possible. No tracking, no profiling, no data mining. Your data stays yours.

2. What We Collect

Only the minimum information necessary for the Service to function. The app does not use analytics, crash reporting, or tracking technologies unless explicitly stated in this Privacy Policy.

  • Camera Feed (Your Device): Dr.Exam accesses your camera for face detection and gaze tracking during proctored exams. All video processing happens locally on your device. No video or image data is uploaded to any server unless you explicitly choose to export logs.
  • Screen Capture (Your Device): Screen recordings are captured and stored locally during proctored sessions. These recordings remain on your device and are never transmitted automatically.
  • Proctoring Logs: Timestamped logs of detections, absences, and alerts are stored locally. These can be exported manually for review.

3. Third-Party Services

Dr.Exam does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The application is fully self-contained.

4. Data Storage

  • Local: All proctoring data, recordings, and logs are stored locally on your device. You have full control. Clear app data or uninstall to remove everything.
  • No Cloud: Dr.Exam currently does not offer cloud storage or cloud sync. All data remains on your machine.

5. Data Security

We take security seriously. All processing is done on-device, minimizing exposure. While no service can guarantee absolute security, keeping data local is the strongest privacy posture available.

6. Your Control

  • Camera & Screen Access: You can revoke permissions at any time through Windows settings.
  • Local Data: Clear app data or uninstall to remove everything from your device immediately.
  • Export Logs: Proctoring logs can be exported manually. You decide what leaves your device.

We may update this policy at any time. Changes take effect when posted. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
